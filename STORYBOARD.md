# Practical Fusion — "Mission 001" landing page storyboard

**Premise.** The visitor is the astronaut. Scrolling is the flight. The site walks them from Armstrong's first step on the Moon, through humanity harnessing the nucleus, to today's uncharted territory, AI, and lands on Practical Fusion as the crew that turns raw power into propulsion.

**Through-line:** *Power was always there. The breakthrough is learning to harness it.*

**Art direction.** Clean line art with flat, two-tone cel shading, inspired by Moebius and Scavengers Reign (but entirely original drawings). Low detail, generous negative space. Deep indigo night built on the logo colour `#180552`, pale lilac line work, one warm "ember" accent `#FFB38A` reserved for the nucleus and moments of energy. Type: Nohemi Regular for slim, large headlines; Nohemi Medium for small uppercase labels.

**Immersion layer (every scene).**
- Cursor movement tilts the whole world a few degrees in 3D (perspective warp), and each illustration layer drifts by its depth: the far sky barely moves, the foreground rock moves most.
- A starfield sits behind everything. It parallaxes with the cursor and stretches into warp streaks when you scroll fast.
- Scenes are a camera fly-through: the next scene approaches small and dim, the current one rushes past and fades.
- A mission timeline (T+00 … T+04) on the right shows where you are; "Skip story" is always available.
- Reduced-motion users get static layers and simple fades.

## Act II — The mission briefing (normal scroll, same universe)

1. **The crew (services).** "Every mission needs a crew." Three cards, which tilt toward the cursor:
   *Tell the story* (Marketing & Communications) · *Build the craft* (Engineering & Development) · *Plot the trajectory* (Go-to-Market). Below them: full-time or fractional, as much or as little as you need.
2. **Frontiers (sectors).** "Five frontiers. One intersection." Semiconductors, AI, energy, quantum and biotech, shown as five line-art planets.
3. **Mission log (portfolio).** Telemetry-style counters: $1B (SMART USA), $80M + $200M+ (national cybersecurity institute), 130+ organizations (national semiconductor conference), $18M+ (public–private research center).
4. **Incoming transmission (testimonial).** David N. Henshall, Semiconductor Research Corporation, with the text typed out like a radio log.
5. **The venture studio.** We invest in mission-driven organizations and innovators.
6. **Open a channel (contact).** First name, last name, email and message, then "Transmit". The footer has the West Palm Beach address, LinkedIn and TwinLabs.
